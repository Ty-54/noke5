Scoring rules and units

  • Long jump: enter feet and inches; converted to centimeters for scoring.
  • Javelin and discus: enter feet and inches; converted to meters for scoring.
  • 200 m: enter seconds. The checkbox applies a +0.24-second adjustment for hand timing, a commonly used conversion for track events up to 400 m. Confirm this rule before competition.
  • 1500 m: enter minutes:seconds, such as 5:30.00. No hand-time adjustment is applied here.
  • Points are rounded down to whole numbers. The five event scores are added for the total.
  • Data is stored in this browser on this device. It is not automatically shared with other devices. Export a backup regularly.
